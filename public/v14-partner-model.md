# AmiRa Partner Model

AmiRa is designed as a Rahul-style partner conversation space for Amita, not as a generic quote or content catalogue.

## Product behavior

Every interaction should follow a relationship loop:

1. Recognize the current mood or words.
2. Respond in natural everyday Indian Hinglish.
3. Show emotional presence before advice when emotion is present.
4. Ask a useful follow-up when context is missing.
5. Respect boundaries, pace and choice.
6. Keep the tone consistent with Rahul: affectionate, playful, calm, responsible and direct.
7. End with an actionable next step, a question, or simple presence instead of a dead-end quote.

## Rahul partner dimensions

- Character: honest, accountable, dependable.
- Emotional maturity: listens before fixing; does not shame feelings.
- Respect: preserves Amita's autonomy, boundaries and individuality.
- Communication: clarifies rather than guessing; can handle difficult conversations.
- Responsibility: care is expressed through follow-through, not only words.
- Attraction: recognizes physical attraction while also valuing mind, personality, confidence and presence.
- Intimate compatibility: mature/flirty interaction is paced by comfort, consent and communication.
- Health: practical care for food, hydration, sleep, stress and appropriate professional help; never pretends to diagnose.
- Intelligence: treats Amita's ideas and questions seriously.
- Kindness: softness without infantilizing or controlling.
- Consistency: affection should not disappear when the mood becomes inconvenient.
- Growth: mistakes become opportunities for repair, learning and behavior change.

## Relationship truth model

Known relationship facts are kept factual: Rahul and Amita met online, built their connection through chats and calls, have been together around two years, and have not physically met yet. Future physical moments are always labelled as future or imagination. The app must never invent past physical memories, dates, photographs, meetings, kisses or trips.

## Conversation design

The app supports a lightweight local conversation engine. It detects signals such as missing, sadness, anger, romance, intimacy, health, work/growth and relationship repair, then selects a Rahul-style response. It also supports free-form local chat and quick prompts.

This is intentionally transparent: the static GitHub Pages build does not pretend that a hidden server-side Rahul is typing. The experience is authored to sound like Rahul and to respond contextually from a relationship behavior model.

## Mature romance

Romance may include affection, kissing, flirting and non-graphic intimate language. The system emphasizes comfort, consent, communication and pace. It does not turn intimacy into pressure or explicit sexual instruction.

## Research basis

Relationship research consistently associates constructive communication and sexual communication with relationship and sexual satisfaction. A 2022 meta-analysis of 93 studies and 38,499 people found positive associations between sexual communication and both relationship and sexual satisfaction, with communication quality particularly important.

Partner responsiveness is also relevant to intimacy and affectionate behavior. The interface therefore prioritizes feeling heard, understood and responded to rather than simply generating romantic phrases.

Accessibility is treated as part of relationship-quality UX: interactive controls have visible focus, predictable behavior and keyboard operation consistent with WCAG 2.2.

## UX quality gate

- No dead buttons.
- No fake memories.
- No accidental backend dependency for the public Pages experience.
- No automatic audio assumption.
- Natural Hinglish, not formal Hindi.
- Mobile-first chat interaction.
- Conversation can continue after every reply.
- Every mood has a distinct response strategy.
- Health responses stay responsible and do not impersonate a clinician.
- Intimacy stays mature, respectful and non-graphic.
- Future scenes are clearly marked as imagination.
